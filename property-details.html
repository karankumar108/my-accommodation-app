About This Thikana

A well-maintained 2-bedroom executive apartment located in Block 13-D Gulshan-e-Iqbal, just 3 minutes walking distance from Disco Bakery and main University Road. The apartment features high ceilings, cross-ventilation windows, full marble flooring, and fitted aluminum balcony railings.

Karachi Utilities Guarantee: Unlike many localities in Karachi, this building is connected to an exempted K-Electric feeder with zero scheduled load-shedding. Sweet KWSB line water is supplied twice weekly directly into the underground reservoir, complemented by a secondary sweet boring pump so tenants never have to order private water tankers.

Suitable strictly for small respectable families or university faculty members. The building is managed by a registered resident union with active 24/7 security guards and night-gate closure.
