Why does Flatora protect tenant contact details?

Traditional Karachi estate brokerages broadcast phone numbers across dozens of unsolicited WhatsApp groups, resulting in harassment—particularly for female students and families. Flatora enforces bilateral privacy: your contact is visible only to the specific verified landlord whose property you request, and only after they formally accept.
