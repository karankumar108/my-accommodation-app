Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how Flatora handles security, utility verification, and visit scheduling.

How does Flatora protect against brokerage commission harassment?
Unlike traditional classifieds or Facebook groups where phone numbers are posted publicly, Flatora keeps all contact information behind an encrypted lock. Landlords and tenants see each other's direct phone and WhatsApp numbers only after the broker explicitly reviews and approves the tenant's application.
Why is the exact street address of Girls Hostels masked?
For student safety, female resident privacy is paramount. Public listings only display the locality (e.g. "PECHS Block 2") and nearby public landmarks. Once a student submits an application and the female warden approves it, the full street address and building number are securely unlocked in the student's My Requests portal.
What does "Approval does not mean Rented" mean?
In Flatora, Approval means the landlord has agreed to share contacts and meet for an on-site physical visit. It does NOT mean the tenant has paid or committed. The broker only changes the status to Rented after an actual physical visit takes place and a tenancy agreement is signed. If either party changes their mind, the broker resets the listing back to Available.
How do I know the real load-shedding schedule of an apartment?
Every property listed on Flatora must declare its specific K-Electric feeder status. Properties are labeled as either Exempted (0 hours), Building Generator Included, or state their daily scheduled outage hours (e.g. Under 2 hours). False declarations lead to broker account suspension by the Karachi Admin.
Can multiple people apply for the same flat at once?
No. Flatora enforces an exclusive lock rule: only ONE active request is permitted per property at any given time. While a property is marked Request Pending, the request button is automatically disabled for all other users to eliminate deceptive bidding wars.
How do brokers register and become verified?
Estate brokers and hostel owners register through the Portal Sign Up, providing their legal name, agency office address in Karachi, and 13-digit NADRA CNIC. The Flatora Admin reviews the credentials before authorizing the broker to upload public listings.
